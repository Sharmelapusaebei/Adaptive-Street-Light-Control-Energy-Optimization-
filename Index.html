Adaptive Street Light Intensity Control & Energy Optimization

System Overview

This edge-native Cyber-Physical System (CPS) replaces static municipal lighting with dynamic sensor fusion (LDR + PIR + DS3231 RTC) on an ATmega328P microcontroller, operating with zero network dependency and sub-200ms latency.

Key Metrics & Objectives

  • 42.6% Energy Reduction: Suppresses unnecessary illumination during 00:00–05:00 off-peak hours.
  • Safety Bias (99.2% Recall): Prioritizes pedestrian safety through zero-memory state-feedback control.
  • Scalable Impact: Projects $149,000/year savings and 55 metric tonnes CO₂ reduction per 10,000 nodes.

Three-Layer Architecture

1. Perception: LDR (10-bit ADC), PIR HC-SR501 (5m range), DS3231 RTC via I2C.

2. Cognitive: Control law $u(t) = S_{\text{atm}} \land (P_{\text{time}} \lor M)$ evaluated every 1 second.

3. Actuator: 5V relay switching a 12V DC LED circuit.

Core System Algorithms

1. Multi-Modal Sensor Fusion Control Law

Combines ambient light ($S_{\text{atm}}$), temporal gating ($P_{\text{time}}$), and PIR motion ($M$):

u(t) = S_atm AND (P_time OR M)

2. Schmitt-Trigger Hysteresis Thresholding

Prevents relay chatter during twilight transitions ($400 \pm 20$ ADC) by establishing stable upper and lower switching boundaries.

3. Binary Classification Control Framework

Validated over 1,000 cycles (TP=495, TN=490, FP=10, FN=5):
