Executive Project Overview & Architecture

The Adaptive Street Light Intensity Control and Energy Optimization System is an edge-native Cyber-Physical System designed to modernize static municipal infrastructure. By integrating ambient light sensing (LDR), passive infrared motion tracking (PIR), and real-time clock controls (RTC), the system implements an automated dual-state operational model: complete deactivation during daytime hours (6 AM–6 PM), low-power 30% baseline illumination at night, and rapid 100% full-intensity transition upon motion detection.

Operational State Matrix

06:00 PM – 06:00 AM No Motion Sensed DIM 30% Baseline Maintain baseline safety illumination without full grid draw.
06:00 PM – 06:00 AM Motion Detected FULL POWER 100% Boost Provide maximum visibility for approaching pedestrians/vehicles.
